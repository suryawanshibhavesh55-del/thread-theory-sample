Thoughtfully Conceived, Meticulously Handcrafted

At THREAD THEORY by Khushboo, every silhouette begins as an intentional study of fabric, drape, and movement. We reject fleeting trends in favor of enduring beauty, creating garments that transition effortlessly between grand celebrations and intimate evenings.

From our atelier in Mumbai, master needle-workers practice age-old embroidery traditions—Zardozi, Marodi, Resham, and Mukaish—reinterpreting heritage crafts for modern sensibilities.
